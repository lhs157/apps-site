# Privacy Policy — Metrics Reader

**Last updated: 2026-11-25 · Contact: luuhongson157@gmail.com**

Metrics Reader is a private, read-only tool used by its developer to view his own AdMob and Google Analytics reports.

- **Data accessed:** AdMob reports and Google Analytics reports of the Google account that signs in, through the read-only scopes `admob.readonly` and `analytics.readonly`.
- **Use:** the data is read on the developer's own computer to review app performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- **Storage:** the sign-in token is stored only on the developer's computer. No server operated by this tool collects or stores the data.
- **Revoking access:** access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Metrics Reader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
